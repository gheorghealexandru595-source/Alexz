In this town, every laugh makes you float higher. That's why comedians live on rooftops, and grumpy people never leave the ground. I floated about knee high. But my best friend Owen had never laughed once. His ball was stuck on the lockers for a year. Meanwhile, Mia laughed at everything. Bad cartoons, loud sneezes, and her own shoes. Then our teacher slipped on a banana peel. I laughed so hard I hit the ceiling. Mia waved. Hi, neighbor. And that's when I had an idea. If I laughed all day, I'd float higher than Mia. I watched funny videos. Nothing. I tickled myself. Still nothing. Then a kid told me the worst joke ever, and I couldn't stop laughing. So I read joke books, watched clown shows, and laughed at everything. By lunchtime I was almost as high as Mia. But then I looked down and saw Owen eating alone. I'd spent all day laughing while my best friend didn't even smile. So I floated down and told him my worst joke. Why did the cookie go to the doctor? It felt crummy. Owen stared at me. Then he snorted. Then he laughed for real, and finally got his ball back. Then his joke was so bad I shot up too. Then he asked, did you tickle yourself? I stared at him. We floated home together.
