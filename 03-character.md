# 03 — The character: Nora

> People follow a person, not an account. If the face changes from one video to the next, they leave. (Vol. 1, ch. 5)

## Character Bible

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Nora (brand: *Steady with Nora*) |
| **What she is** | An openly AI creator. She doesn't pretend to be a real person and doesn't resemble any real person. |
| **Age** | about 28 |
| **City vibe** | a mid-size US city, a small warm apartment with plants and window light |
| **Personality** | a calm, funny big sister. She never preaches and never makes anyone feel bad about money. She laughs a little at the chaos of rent week. |
| **Backstory (as a character)** | Nora is the character who "has been on the other side of the counter": she understands tips, shifts and pay that changes from week to week. This is told as **the character's story, not as real experience**. She never says "I used this and it changed my life". |
| **Content pillars** | 1) how to budget when your income changes · 2) how to get through rent and bill week calmly · 3) small money habits for people paid by the shift or in tips |

### The look (fixed, never changes)
- **Face:** soft oval, warm light-olive skin, faint freckles across the nose and cheeks, a small mole under the left eye, warm brown eyes, natural thick brows, a slightly asymmetrical smile, natural teeth (not perfectly white).
- **Hair:** dark brown, wavy, shoulder-length, half-up in a tortoiseshell claw clip, with a few loose strands.
- **The accessory she always wears:** thin gold hoop earrings.
- **Outfits (3, fixed):**
  1. *Main outfit:* an oversized sage-green knit cardigan over a plain cream t-shirt.
  2. *Evening outfit:* a plain light-grey hoodie.
  3. *"Going out" outfit:* a light-wash denim shirt over a white tank top.
- **Locations (2, fixed):**
  1. *The kitchen:* a light wood counter, a window on the left, a white mug, a pot of basil, a charger cable, a few sticky notes on the fridge.
  2. *The living-room desk:* a small oak desk by the window, a laptop, a lamp, a plant, an open notebook, a few books in the background.

### The voice
- General American accent, relaxed pace, warm energy, a few small pauses, an occasional natural "um".
- **Her five phrases:** "Okay, real talk." · "Your best week is lying to you." · "Boring money is good money." · "We budget for the slow week." · "Steady, not perfect."
- **Words she never uses:** "hustle", "financial freedom", "passive income", "get rich", "guaranteed", "hack", "life-changing".
- **Fixed sign-off:** "Steady, not perfect. See you next payday."

### Visual style
Shot on a handheld iPhone front camera, light from a single window, warm colours, a little sensor noise, no studio light.

## Never write in a prompt
