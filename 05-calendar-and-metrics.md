> **Watch the clock change:** on October 25, 2026 Europe switches to winter time, but the US only on November 1. In the week of October 25 – November 1 the difference with New York is **6 hours**, not 7, so schedule one hour earlier (13:30 / 19:00 / 01:30–02:30). After November 1 it's back to 7 hours.

## The numbers to track every week (Vol. 1, ch. 10)

| What you measure | Starting target | If it's below target |
|------------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Still watching after 3 seconds | over 65% | a more specific hook, movement in the first second, big text on the first frame |
| Watch to the end | 25–35% | faster cuts, remove a block from the middle (but stay over 60 s) |
| Profile visits | over 1% of views | say clearly at the end what's in the bio |
| Link clicks | over 10% of profile visits | link straight to the free version |
| Buy from the page | 2–5% | first image, price, FAQ |
| Free → paid | 3–8% within 14 days | better emails, a coupon with a deadline |

**The Monday question (Vol. 2):** where are people getting stuck? Pick the narrowest spot and work only on it for a week.
