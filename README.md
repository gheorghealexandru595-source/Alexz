## Three things to know first (double-checked)

**1. TikTok Creator Rewards (pay per view) is not available to accounts based in Romania.**
Every list found in October 2026 includes only a handful of countries (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Brazil; some sources add Canada, Mexico, Italy, Spain or Australia). The sources disagree with each other and none of them is TikTok's official page, but Romania is on none of them. The basic requirements are: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, a personal account, original videos longer than one minute.
Do not use a VPN or a foreign SIM to "move" the account (Vol. 1, ch. 8). It breaks the rules and can get the whole account restricted.
**What I did:** every video is still over one minute (72 seconds), so you are ready if the program opens in Romania or if you set up a business in an eligible country, and videos over one minute perform well anyway. The main income comes from the **digital product on Payhip**, which works from Romania (Vol. 1, ch. 1). For Facebook, check Professional Dashboard → Monetization; Meta's program is invitation-based and I could not find a list that confirms Romania.

**2. The character is an openly AI creator.**
Turn on the "AI-generated" label on every TikTok post and the equivalent AI label on Instagram and Facebook. The bio says "AI creator". Nora never says she used the product or that it changed her life; she shows the product on screen and explains it (FTC rules in the US, ASA rules in the UK).

**3. The niche is personal budgeting, with no investment advice.**
No income promises and no investment advice, only organizing everyday money.

## What to do today (in order)

1. Read `01` and `02`. Build the product with the prompts in `02` (2–4 hours with Claude + Google Sheets).
2. Open a Payhip account (free), connect Stripe or PayPal, upload the product and the free version.
3. Generate Nora's portrait in Google Flow with prompt 3.2 in `03`. Make 8–12 variations, keep one and save it as an **Ingredient**.
4. Create the accounts with the names and bios in `04` and upload the logo or the portrait.
5. Make the first video from `videos/video-01.md` and schedule it for the US evening (02:00–05:00 Romania time).
