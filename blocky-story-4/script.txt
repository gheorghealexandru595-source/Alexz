In this town, every time you get angry, your head gets bigger. That's why grumpy people can't fit through doors, and calm people have heads like grapes. My head was pretty normal. But my best friend Max had the biggest head in school. He hadn't worn a hat in three years. Meanwhile, Ivy never got mad. Stepped on her foot? Smile. Juice on her sweater? Smile. Took her seat? Still smiling. Then someone ate my pudding. My head blew up like a balloon. Ivy gasped. And that's when I had an idea. If I stayed calm all day, I'd have a tiny head like Ivy. I counted to ten. Nothing. I tried yoga. Still nothing. Then a kid spilled milk on me, and I just laughed. My head shrank right back. So I let everything go. Someone cut in line? Fine. Bad grade? Fine. By the end of the day, my head was tiny. But then I saw Max, stuck in a door, alone. I'd spent all day staying calm while my best friend was stuck. So I pulled him free. What's wrong? Everything makes me mad. Just forgive one thing. He thought really hard. I forgive the door. And my little brother. For the first time in three years, Max wore a hat. Also, I ate your pudding. My head started to grow. I forgave him. Mostly.
