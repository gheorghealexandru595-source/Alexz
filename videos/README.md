## Flow credits

9 blocks × 2 variations × ~20 credits (Veo 3.1 Fast) ≈ **360 credits per video**. With Google AI Pro (1,000 credits a month) that covers about 2–3 complete videos a month. To make more:

- generate **one** variation and redo only the failed blocks;
- reuse the closing block (scene 9) and the "I'm an AI creator" block across several videos;
- use more real screen recordings (they cost nothing) and fewer seconds with Nora;
- move to Ultra only when revenue justifies it (the Vol. 2 rule: tools stay under 15% of revenue).

> The credit costs come from the guide (Vol. 1, ch. 6). I could not confirm them on Google's page for October 2026, so check them in the app before paying.
