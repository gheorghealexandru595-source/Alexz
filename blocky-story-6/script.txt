In this town, every time you worry, you get slower. That's why calm kids zoom around, and worriers move like snails. I was pretty average. But my best friend Ben was the slowest kid in school. He'd never once made it to the bus on time. Meanwhile, Kai never worried. Pop quiz? Done. Fire drill? First out. Lunch line? Already eating. Then our teacher said, test tomorrow. I started worrying, and slowed to half speed. Kai lapped me twice. And that's when I had an idea. If I stopped worrying, I'd be fast like Kai. I told myself, don't worry. Still slow. I tried running. Even slower. Then a kid asked me to play tag, and I forgot all about the test. I zoomed off. So I kept busy. I played tag, jumped rope, and threw paper airplanes. By lunch, I was almost as fast as Kai. But then I saw Ben, still opening his milk, alone. I'd spent all day zooming while my best friend was stuck in slow motion. I sat down. What's wrong? What if I miss the bus again? Then we'll walk. Together. He smiled, a little faster. Then he laughed. Full speed. And for the first time ever, Ben caught the bus. Then he said, wait, test tomorrow? We both slowed down. So we studied on the bus.
