In this town, every lie you tell makes you shrink. That's why honest people duck under doorframes, and liars get stepped on. I was about average. But my best friend Sam was the size of a water bottle. He needed a stool to reach the water fountain. Meanwhile, Nora never lied. Ugly sweater? She said so. Burnt cookies? She said so. Bad singing? Also said so. Then our teacher asked who drew on the board. I said, not me. I shrank two inches. Nora raised an eyebrow. And that's when I had an idea. If I only told the truth, I'd be tall like Nora. The sky is blue. Nothing. Water is wet. Still nothing. Then I told the teacher it was me. I grew right back. So I told the truth all day. I hate broccoli. I'm scared of ducks. I sleep with a teddy bear. By lunch, I was almost as tall as Nora. But then I saw Sam, sitting in a lunchbox, alone. I'd spent all day growing while my best friend kept shrinking. So I asked, why do you lie? So people like me. I like the real you. He took a deep breath. I hate my haircut. And I can't swim. For the first time, Sam drank from the fountain by himself. Then he said, I drew on the board too. I stared at him. So we told the teacher. Together.
