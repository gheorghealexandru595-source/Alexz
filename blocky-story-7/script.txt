In this town, every secret you keep makes you see-through. That's why blabbermouths are rock solid, and secret keepers are basically ghosts. I was pretty solid. But my best friend Jonah was so faint you could read through him. Our teacher had marked him absent for a month. Meanwhile, Bella had zero secrets. Her crush? Everyone knew. Her diary? Read it out loud. Her password? Bella one two three. Then I broke my mom's favorite vase, and hid the pieces. I went see-through. Mom walked right past me. And that's when I had an idea. If I spilled all my secrets, I'd be solid like Bella. I told my goldfish. Nothing. I wrote it in my diary. Still nothing. Then my mom asked about the vase, and I told her the truth. I went solid again. So I told everyone everything. I can't whistle. I'm scared of pigeons. I cried at a cartoon. By lunch, I was as solid as Bella. But then I almost sat on Jonah. He was almost gone, eating alone. I'd spent all day spilling secrets while my best friend was disappearing. What are you hiding? I'm moving away next month. And I'm scared you'll forget me. I could never forget you. The next day at roll call, Jonah? Here! Then he said, also, I took your pencil. I stared at him. Best month ever.
