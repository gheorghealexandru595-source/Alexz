In this town, every happy birthday you get pops a balloon out of your head. Get enough, and you float. That's why popular kids float to their parties, and forgotten kids walk. Today was my birthday. I was hoping for ten balloons. But my best friend Oscar had the same birthday, and nobody ever remembered. He'd never blown out a single candle. Meanwhile, Bianca had so many balloons she needed a seatbelt. She floated to school. She floated to lunch. She floated into a tree. Then my grandma called. Happy birthday, sweetie. Pop. My first balloon. And that's when I had an idea. If I got enough wishes, I could fly over the whole town. I wished myself happy birthday. Nothing. I wore a sign that said, birthday boy. People just read it. Then I held the door for the whole class, and they all sang to me. Pop, pop, pop. Eight balloons. The bus driver honked it. The lunch lady sang it. Even my dog barked it. By sunset, I was floating over the street. Then I looked down and saw Oscar, on his steps. Zero balloons. Same birthday. Nobody remembered. I'd spent all day collecting wishes while my best friend got none. So I floated down and gave him all my balloons. Happy birthday, Oscar. Then I yelled, it's his birthday too! Pop, pop, pop. That night, Oscar blew out his first candles. Then he looked at me and said, happy birthday. Pop. Best birthday ever.
