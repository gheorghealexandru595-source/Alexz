In this town, every picture you take makes another you. That's why famous kids walk around in crowds of themselves, and camera-shy people only ever have one. I had exactly two of me. But my best friend Benny had never been in a single photo. Meanwhile, Jade had forty-two. One to carry her bag, one to hold her smoothie, and one just to clap. Then on picture day, the photographer snapped me by accident. Pop. Three of me. Everyone gasped. And that's when I had an idea. If I got enough pictures, I'd be a crowd like Jade. I posed by the lockers. Nobody noticed. I did a backflip. Still nothing. Then a kid dropped his books, so I helped him pick them up, and everyone took my picture. Pop. Pop. Pop. So I started holding doors, carrying trays, and cheering for everyone. By lunchtime there were twelve of me. But then I noticed Benny eating alone. Just one of him. I'd spent all day collecting myself and ignored my best friend. I sat down. Benny, smile. I hate pictures. Just one. Click. Click. For once, Benny had a full table. Then he grabbed my phone and snapped me right back. Then his copy said, nice backflip. I stared at him. Best picture I ever took.
