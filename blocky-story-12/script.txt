In this town, every smile you give someone makes you a little lighter. Give enough, and you float. That's why happy people drift over the rooftops, and grumpy people stomp around on the sidewalk. I hovered about knee high. But my best friend Theo never smiled. Not once. He'd never seen the fireworks from up high. Meanwhile, Daisy smiled so much she had to tie herself to a mailbox. She smiled at dogs. She smiled at mailmen. She smiled at her own reflection. Then the mailman waved at me, and I smiled back. Whoosh. I floated a whole foot. And that's when I had an idea. If I smiled enough, I'd watch the fireworks from the sky. I smiled at a mirror. Nothing. I forced the biggest smile ever. My face just hurt. Then a little girl dropped her ice cream, so I gave her mine. She smiled. I smiled. Whoosh. So I smiled at everyone. The whole street. The bus driver. A very confused cat. By sunset, I was floating over the rooftops. Then I looked down and saw Theo, alone on the ground, staring up. I'd spent all day smiling at strangers while my best friend couldn't even leave the ground. So I floated down and asked, why don't you smile? He mumbled, my braces. Everyone laughs. So I made the goofiest face I could. He laughed. Then he smiled, braces and all. And for the first time, Theo watched the fireworks from the sky. Then he said, your goofy face is still the worst. I stared at him. Then we both smiled, and floated even higher.
