In this town, the night GTA 6 came out, everybody stopped everything. Every window glowed blue. Every kid had a controller. Everybody except me. Because I worked the night shift at KFC. While everyone else was stealing cars, I was stealing fries from the fryer. My best friend Marco called. Bro, you're missing everything. I said, I'm frying chicken. A kid came in for a bucket, still holding his controller. He asked if I'd seen the map. I had not seen the map. Even my manager was playing it behind the fryer. I fried chicken for six hours straight. And that's when the storm hit. Boom. The whole town went dark. Every TV. Every console. Every single save file. Except one building. Our fryers ran on a generator. So the whole town showed up. Hungry. Bored. Lost without their game. And for one night, I was the most popular kid in town. I served wings to my teacher. Fries to the mayor. A bucket to Marco, who looked kind of guilty. He said, sorry I made fun of your job. I said, sit down. I've got a break. We ate chicken by candlelight and everyone traded stories. Somebody even brought a guitar. Nobody looked at a screen once. When the power came back, nobody even moved. Then Marco whispered, don't worry, I'll tell you how the game ends. I threw a drumstick at him. Best launch night ever.
