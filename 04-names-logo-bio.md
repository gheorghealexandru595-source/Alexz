# 04 — Names, logo and bios

## Brand name: **Steady with Nora**

**Why this name:** "steady" is exactly what the buyer wants (calm money even though income is irregular), it's a simple word anyone in the US and UK understands, and "with Nora" ties it to the character. It has no "rich", "money hacks" or income promises, so it doesn't attract platform policy trouble.

## Logo / profile picture

Files: [`brand/logo.svg`](brand/logo.svg) (vector, scales without loss) and [`brand/logo-1080.png`](brand/logo-1080.png) (1080×1080, ready to upload).

**What it means:** the white line starts as a zig-zag (irregular income) and becomes straight and calm (a steady budget), ending in a terracotta dot. The sage-green circle fits the round profile-picture crop on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

**Brand colours** (use them in Canva, in the product and in captions):
| Colour | Hex | Where |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Sage | `#8FA98B` | logo background, "green = fine" cells |
| Cream | `#F4EFE6` | backgrounds, text on sage |
| Terracotta | `#C0563A` | accent, arrows, the keyword in captions |
| Charcoal | `#2B2B2B` | text |

**Fonts (Canva):** headings *Playfair Display* or *Georgia*, body *Inter*. CapCut captions: bold white with a black outline, the key word in terracotta.

### The face version (recommended after the first 2 weeks)
For character creators, a face photo usually converts better than a logo, because people follow a person. Start with the logo while you test the face; once Nora is final, use the prompt below for the profile picture and keep the logo on Payhip and as a small watermark.
```
Using the reference image, keep exactly the same face, freckles, mole, hair, clip, earrings and skin. Close-up head and shoulders portrait of Nora, an original fictional woman, smiling warmly at the camera, in the oversized sage-green knit cardigan over a cream t-shirt. Plain soft cream background (#F4EFE6), soft window light from the left, visible skin texture, natural teeth, phone-camera look. Face centred with space around it so it fits a circle crop. 1:1 square, no text, no logos, no watermark.
```
